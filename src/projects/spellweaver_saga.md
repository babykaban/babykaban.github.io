
## Introduction
Spellweaver Saga is a roguelike adventure where players explore islands created after a cataclysm, seeking to restore the world. Using a deep spell crafting system, everything from combat to puzzles revolves around elemental magic.

## Gameplay & Features
### Spell Crafting System
- Players manipulate Fire (F), Water (W), Air (A), and Earth (E)—called spheres.
- The number of spell slots increases as players progress, allowing for more complex spell combinations.
- Spells affect combat, puzzles, and the environment dynamically.
- Players gather spheres during exploration to expand their magical arsenal.

### Exploration & Procedural World
- Key locations are handcrafted, while some islands are procedurally generated.
- The world is mystical and ever-changing, shaped by the player's discoveries.

### Sanctum (Player’s Base)
- A hidden sanctuary that provides resources and power.
- Used as a launch point for exploration.
- If the shrine takes too much damage, the game ends permanently.

### Progression & Survival
- Level up by gaining experience, upgrading the base, and finding rare spheres.
- Death is punishing—players lose some spheres, and the shrine takes damage.
- Strategic planning is key to long-term survival and world restoration.


## Development & Technology
- Developed in C/C++ using a custom engine.
- Features a custom editor to design handcrafted locations and manage procedural generation.
